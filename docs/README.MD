# MTG Documents

## Introduction

Unfortunately, creating and running any group of people/activities requires management and administration!

This README catalogues the processes and documents used in the group management.

Note: This is a very dynamic document, so should be referenced and updated frequently!

## MTG Guilds and Charter

- Guild leaders are encouraged to create/amend infographics for their guilds, to give an accurate view to potential members of the nature and ambitions of the Guild.  These graphics will be updatd on the website and displaed at meetups.
- The Guild charter is an important document which is intended to spell out our intentions and capabilities and set limitations on the liabiltiy of our group and members.  Safety, legality and security a prime committments in the charter.  The charter should always be visible in the meetings.

## MTG Locations

### Library at the Doc

An MOU is created annually between MTG and the Library
For 2026:  MTG was represented by Mick Havill and the Library by Alex Minenna (<Alex.Minenna@melbourne.vic.gov>)
A copy of the MOU and supporting documents should be attached HERE

MTG is required to provide information to the library in preparation of the MOU and then on a monthly basis.

Annually:

- A statement about the  group, organisation, members and ambitions
- Signed copy of MOU and supporting documents
- Copies of Working With Children Checks
- Organisers that have carried out the induction(s) necessary to use the Library facilities
- A commitment to maintain the safety and security of the facilities
- In 2026 we have moved from the Performance Space to Meeting 2.  We may request a change if we find that the space in Meeting 2 is not suitable for us as the group changes.

The Library may use any of the information provided to market the Library.
They have an entry for MTG in the Melbourne City Council  [What&#39;s On Melbourne](https://whatson.melbourne.vic.gov.au/)

Mick has a login to the account to be able to update the content when required.

Monthly:

- On the day of the event an inducted member need to pick up  and sign for the meeting room access card in advance of the meeting time (opens at 12:00pm).  We should ensure that there are always at least two inducted members at the meeting.
- After the meeting, within the week, a short email should be sent to Alex giving approximate numbers in attendance for each event.
- Some photos are always welcomed
- Any issues with the facility, staff, access etc.(generally none)

Ad-Hoc:

- Any changes/cancellations to arrangements should be flagged to Alex ASAP to allow them to re-use the space.
- Any security issues arising should be brought to the security staff IMMEDIATELY.

### Preparations for monthly meetings

#### Before the meeting

##### *Luma*

- check event is listed on Luma correctly
- add hosts
- check numbers
- encourage non attendees to cancel (blast/email)
- Consider moving waiting list to approved
- Attendee list with sign-in for new members

##### *Newsletters/emails (Mautic)*

- send a week or two beforehand

##### *Discord Announcements*

- duplicate or link in #announcements channel
- generate excitement

#### On the day

- Collect pass and sign-in with Library
- Post direction signs (BlueTac) on second floor
- Guild posters (BlueTac) in meeting room on wall
- Charter loose copy (BlueTac) in meeting room on wall
- Badges, pre-printed for previous members or blank with written names for new
- Sign-in and Photo release- IMPORTANT for new members having read the charter (PROTECTS US!)
- Take photos/videos

#### Follow Up

- Update email list with sign-ins (Mautic) and file hardcopy
- Cut badges for new members
- Feedback to Alex Minnena
- Feedback to attendees? As a newsletter
