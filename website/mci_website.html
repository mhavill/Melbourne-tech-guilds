Who We Are

🛠️ Community of Makers

We're many engineers, technicians, and creative makers with expertise spanning robotics, electronics, mechanics, coding, and deep tech. With hundreds of combined years of industry experience, we share knowledge freely.

🤝 Support for Your Projects

Stuck on your project? We're here to help. Get advice on your Arduino, ESP32, or Raspberry Pi builds. Learn best practices for version control, backups, and design. We guide and encourage you to complete your projects—and you'll discover new interests along the way.

🤝 Your Tribe of Makers

Finally, people who speak your language! Whether you're debugging code or designing a chassis, we understand the challenges. Share your projects, get unstuck when frustrated, discover new technologies to explore. Most members work on their own projects with guild support—and many find unexpected interests along the way.

💡 Free Knowledge & Support

Advice is always free. Materials are cheap and often available. Need experience for your resume? Collaborate on real projects. Looking for employment or manufacturing contacts? Leverage our networks.

Our Guilds

Each guild is a support community for specific technologies. Bring your project challenges, learn best practices, and discover new interests. Most members focus on their own projects with guild support—collaboration opportunities emerge naturally.
