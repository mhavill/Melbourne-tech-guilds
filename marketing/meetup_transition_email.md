# 🎉 New Year, New Name, Same Awesome Community!

**Happy Holidays from your fellow makers!** 🛠️

We're excited to share some big news as we head into 2026...

**After many years of hosting events with Meetup we are:**

1. Moving to new event hosting platforms away from Meetup
2. Restructuring and rebranding to better reflect the breadth of technology and interests we have as a group
3. Collaborating with other technology community groups in Melbourne and nationally

## 🚀 We've Evolved!

**Introducing: Melbourne Tech Guilds**

We've outgrown our Raspberry Pi roots! While we still love RPi, we've expanded to embrace the full spectrum of tech and engineering that our community is passionate about:

🤖 **Robotics** • ⚙️ **Mechanics** • 📡 **IoT** • 💻 **Coding** • 🔬 **Deep Dive**

*Still for hobbyists. Still about building cool stuff. Just... MORE of it.*
