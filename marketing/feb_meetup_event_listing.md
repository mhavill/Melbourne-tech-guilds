# Monthly Meeting in Library at the Dock

## Build. Share. Learn. Collaborate.

**Join Melbourne's community of makers, engineers, and tech hobbyists for our monthly meetup!**

---

## 📅 When

**Sunday, 1st February 2026**
**12:30 PM - 4:30 PM**

## 📍 Where

**Library at the Dock**
107 Victoria Harbour Promenade, Docklands
Meeting room 1, Second Floor (near the Makerspace)

---

## 🎯 What to Expect

**Show & Tell** - Bring your projects (finished or gloriously unfinished!)
**Problem Solving** - Stuck on something? Get help from experienced makers
**Knowledge Sharing** - Learn new techniques and technologies
**Chat** - Meet fellow creators who speak your language

---

## 🤖 Our Tech Guilds

Whether you're into one or all of these, you'll find your people:

• **Robotics** - AI/ML, sensors, autonomous systems
• **Mechanics** - 3D printing, CNC, CAD/CAM
• **IoT** - ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, home automation
• **Coding** - Embedded systems, Python, C/C++
• **Deep Dive** - Electronics, applied maths, engineering theory

---

## 👥 Who Should Come?

✅ Hobbyists working on personal projects
✅ Anyone stuck on a technical problem
✅ People curious about new technologies
✅ Makers looking for project inspiration
✅ Engineers wanting to connect with the community

**All skill levels welcome** - from "just getting started" to "20 years experience"

---

## 💡 What to Bring

- Your current project (if you want feedback)
- Questions you're stuck on
- Your laptop/device (optional)
- An open mind and curiosity

**No preparation required** - just show up!

---

## 🌟 Why Join Us?

⚡ **Free advice and mentorship** from experienced makers
🔧 **Hands-on help** with your specific challenges
🤝 **Connections** with Melbourne's maker community
💡 **Discover new interests** - come for ESP32, leave excited about 3D printing
🎉 **Friendly, judgment-free zone** - we've all had projects that don't work!
